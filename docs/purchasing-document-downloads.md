# Vendor RFQ and supplier purchase-order documents

## Scope and direction

Mehta is the issuer; an existing active Purchasing vendor is the recipient. These are CRM purchasing documents. They do not create Tally vouchers, reserve stock, post goods receipts, send messages, or change the existing RFQ comparison and operational PO/GRN workflows. The document pages are separate from those operational records and do not claim to have issued an inventory PO.

References reviewed: 10925-2601084 (1).pdf, 1250031480 (1).pdf, AOB PO (2).pdf are incoming customer purchase orders; 10226-2707445 (1).pdf is a supplier sales-order acknowledgment; 10626-2700426.pdf is a supplier invoice; 426-Jain_Courier-23240.pdf is a courier document; KGEP standard sales terms belong to KGEP. Their field structure informed the layout. No customer/supplier branding, numbering, bank details or commercial/legal wording was copied into settings or templates.

## Pages and setup

- Supply > Vendor RFQ documents: `/purchasing/rfqs`.
- Supply > Supplier PO documents: `/purchasing/purchase-orders`.
- Document settings are shared across both pages. Editing requires the existing `rules.manage` permission. Reading pages also requires `purchasing.read`.
- Choose existing permission keys separately for create, approve, download and record-external-send for each type. Grants are managed through Users & Roles. There are no role-name checks in this document service. Empty authority blocks the corresponding action, even for accounts with wildcard access.
- Company name, GSTIN/state, address, contact, footer, optional PNG logo, purchasing terms and optional withholding text are stored in `company_document_settings`. Values start empty. Tamil Nadu state code 33 must be explicitly saved. Logo is limited to a 200 KB PNG; a placeholder is printed when absent.
- Configure a prefix for each kind. Number format is `{prefix}/{April-to-March financial year}/{six-digit minimum counter}`. For example, the fictional test prefix `DEMO-RFQ` produces `DEMO-RFQ/2026-27/000001`. Counters are transactionally serialized per kind/year; cancelled documents keep their numbers. A retry UUID prevents duplicate creation on retries. Prefix format needs business confirmation.
- Once numbered, a draft's date may change within its financial year. Moving it to a different financial year is blocked; cancel it and create a new draft so the date and allocated number remain consistent.
- Draft downloads default OFF. Enabling the option permits DRAFT-watermarked downloads/previews of drafts; pending and cancelled documents remain blocked. Approved/sent PDFs use the approved snapshot. Settings changes require a new review of an unsent approval. Sent documents retain their issued snapshot and remain accessible to currently authorised downloaders.

## Business workflow

1. Register an actual supplier through existing Purchasing > Vendors, including address, state and GSTIN. Search it on the document form; do not enter a customer as the supplier.
2. Create an RFQ with date, response deadline, requested lead time, requested price/delivery/payment/validity terms and unpriced item lines. Code, description, quantity and UoM are required; make is available. No terms are generated by the software.
3. Create a PO with supplier, date, delivery date, bill-to, ship-to, payment, freight/packing, item code/description/make/HSN/UoM/quantity, list unit price, discount and GST. Enter zero explicitly when applicable. Set the rounding checkbox deliberately. Terms are entered or copied from the user-configured settings, never from reference attachments.
4. Save Draft. Open the row to inspect status and total or edit a draft. Supply a reason for every workflow/PDF action.
5. Submit for approval. Company identity/contact and supplier address must be complete. POs also need supplier GSTIN, HSN, addresses, payment/freight terms, purchasing terms and signatory.
6. A different currently authorised reviewer opens the same page, selects the pending document and chooses Approve. Purchasing documents always keep independent review, including Owner-created documents. The quotation-only Owner exception does not apply here.
7. Preview or download the approved PDF. Required authority is checked on the server. Before sending, the approving account and role must remain active and authorised. Return an approved document to draft for a new review if necessary. After recording Sent, its independent approval evidence and issued snapshot are retained; later staff or company-setting changes do not rewrite that historical document. Downloaders still need current download authority.
8. After actually sending the document outside ControlDesk, choose Confirm sent outside ControlDesk. This records an audit event only; it does not send any email or WhatsApp message. Cancel is available before Sent. No hard-delete endpoint exists.

## Calculation conventions

Uses the existing shared quotation decimal arithmetic: non-negative decimal strings with at most six decimal places, BigInt intermediate arithmetic, half-up paise rounding per line/tax component. Line amount uses the full discounted unit calculation; displayed net rate is separately rounded to paise. Mehta buyer/destination state is 33. Supplier origin 33 gives CGST and SGST; another valid supplier state gives IGST. This module implements the specified ordinary domestic supplier-to-Mehta case; special place-of-supply scenarios require confirmation before use.

Optional final nearest-rupee rounding is a separate signed adjustment. It does not modify GST. Amount in words uses the existing Indian rupee/lakh/crore formatter. Freight/packing and withholding are text fields, not unconfirmed automatic charge/deduction formulas. PDFs are purchase documents, not supplier tax invoices.

| Independent review | Creator submits and tries to approve via UI/API: blocked. A different account with the configured approval permission can approve. |

| Revisions | Return pending/approved document to Draft; edit and resubmit. Version increases, prior approval is cleared. A stale version receives 409. |
| Numbering | Create multiple documents and retry the exact creation request: one document per retry UUID; distinct sequential numbers; 31 March and 1 April use separate FY counters. Do not change the production clock. |
| Download/preview | PDF opens in the preview and downloads with a safe filename. Check issuer/recipient direction, all rows, GST, total, words, terms, signatory and page numbering. Review a multipage document. |

| Revocation | Remove download authority and retry: current actor must be blocked. Disable the earlier approver: an unsent approved download/send must require renewed review. Historical Sent documents retain independent approval evidence and remain downloadable by currently authorised users. |

## Needs confirmation before live issuance

1. Mehta GSTIN and registered address: attachments contain a candidate identity, but settings intentionally remain empty until confirmed for these documents.
2. Mehta legal name/contact, logo and footer.
3. Approved purchasing terms; no legal/commercial text has been authored on Mehta's behalf.
4. Number prefixes, financial-year representation, padding and starting sequence. Current configurable format uses April-March FY and six-digit minimum padding; arbitrary historical starting counters are not exposed.
5. Approval/create/download/send permission assignments and eligible roles; independent approval remains mandatory for purchasing.
6. Draft-download policy, initially OFF.
7. Whether a tax-withholding line is required and its confirmed wording/calculation. Current optional line is informational only, with no deduction.
8. Whether to show list price and discount or net rate only; explicit settings toggle, initially false.
9. Special tax/place-of-supply cases, multiple delivery states, freight/packing amounts or withholding deductions require an agreed calculation design before being used.
